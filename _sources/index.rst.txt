Book Codex
----------

:doc:`On Growth and Form (1917) & Dynamic Symmetry Companion (1919–1920) <growth_and_form/index>`
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*On Growth and Form*, First Edition (Cambridge: University Press, 1917, xv + 793 pp., 17 chapters) · *The Diagonal* (Vol. I, Nos. 1–12) & *Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase* (New Haven: Yale University Press, 1919–1920).
