Kant
====

**Immanuel Kant** (1724 – 1804) — *German Philosopher*
   Quoted by Thompson at the opening of Chapter I: 'in any special doctrine of nature there can be only so much proper science as there is mathematics therein' (Metaphysische Anfangsgründe der Naturwissenschaft, 1786)—the programmatic challenge answered by On Growth and Form.
