Meyer
=====

**Georg Hermann von Meyer** (1815 – 1892) — *German Anatomist at the University of Zürich*
   Published Die Architektur der Spongiosa (1867), demonstrating with Karl Culmann that cancellous bone lamellae align along orthogonal principal tension and compression curves rather than arbitrary anatomical lattices (Chapter XVI).
