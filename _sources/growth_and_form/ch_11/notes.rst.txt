========================================================

.. rubric:: Scholarly Apparatus & GEOMETOR Cross-References — Ch. XI

Scholarly Commentary & GEOMETOR Concordance (Chapter XI)
--------------------------------------------------------

Gnomonic Self-Similarity Across `geometor/aristotle`, `geometor/euclid`, and `geometor/divine`
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Whirling Squares and Dynamic Symmetry**: Directly anticipates :person:`Jay Hambidge's <hambidge>` Dynamic Symmetry (:cite:`hambidge:1920-diagonal`, 1919–1920).
* **Why *Nautilus* Is Not Golden**: Thompson clarifies that *any* constant ratio of tangential to radial growth produces an equiangular spiral; *Nautilus* has $\alpha \approx 80^\circ$, not $\phi$.

Structured Concordance Loci in This Chapter
-------------------------------------------

**Spira Mirabilis, Hero's Theory of the Gnomon, Golden Triangles, and Turbinated Shells** (*Ch. XI (The Logarithmic Spiral)*, pp. 493–586)
   Reveals why Nautilus, ammonites, gastropods, foraminifera, claws, and horns all obey the equiangular spiral: accretion of dead shell or horn at a growing margin without changing shape is mathematically equivalent to gnomonic self-similarity.
   *Algebraic Invariants:* ``r = a e^(θ cot α)`` · ``φ² = φ + 1`` · ``φ = (1 + √5)/2``

   * **geometor/euclid** (*Book II, Def. 2 & Prop. 11; Book IV, Prop. 10; Book VI, Prop. 30*): Generalizes Euclid's parallelogrammic gnomon (II Def. 2) via Hero Def. 58 to the whirling-square golden rectangle (II.11, VI.30) and the 36°–72°–72° golden triangle (IV.10).
   * **geometor/aristotle** (*Categories 15a29–33*): Recovers Aristotle's law that gnomonic accretion increases magnitude without altering form (αὐξάνεται μὲν, ἀλλοιοῦται δ' οὔ).
   * **geometor/divine** (*Golden Ratio φ = (1 + √5)/2 & Gnomonic Chains*): Thompson's Figs. 252 and 254 provide the classical morphological realization of recursive φ gnomons.
   * *Dynamic Symmetry Parallel:* The Diagonal (1919–1920), Lesson I & Whirling Squares — direct citation of logarithmic shell growth and square gnomons of the φ rectangle
