Chapter XIV: On Leaf-Arrangement, or Phyllotaxis — Summary¶

Analytical Synopsis — 1917 Cambridge First Edition — Chapter XIV (pp. 635–651)

Overview of Chapter XIV (On Leaf-Arrangement, or Phyllotaxis)¶

Chapter XIV presents Thompson’s classic analysis of botanical phyllotaxis, continued fractions, and parastichies:

  1. Helical Stems vs. Discoidal Capitula (pp. 635–638): Unifies cylindrical stem phyllotaxis with planar sunflower inflorescences (Schimper, Braun, Bravais, bravais:1837; Church, church:1904).

  2. Schimper–Braun Series & Continued Fractions (pp. 638–642): Analyzes the divergence series $1/2, 1/3, 2/5, 3/8, 5/13, 8/21, \dots$ converging to the golden divergence angle $\phi^{-2} \approx 0.381966$ ($137^\circ 30' 28''$).

  3. P. G. Tait’s Parastichy Lattice (pp. 643–646): Details Peter Guthrie Tait's theorem (tait:1872) that intersecting $(m, n)$ contact parastichies necessarily generate intermediate $m + n$ helices.

  4. Mechanical Close-Packing (pp. 646–651): Rejects mystical teleology in favor of Hofmeister's mechanical gap-rule (hofmeister:1868) and physical pressure.
