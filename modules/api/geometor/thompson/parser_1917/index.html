geometor.thompson.parser_1917¶

Deterministic parser for the 1917 Cambridge First Edition of On Growth and Form.

Transforms the Distributed Proofreaders / Project Gutenberg transcription (#55264) of D’Arcy Wentworth Thompson’s 1917 Cambridge University Press First Edition into clean, zero-warning Sphinx reStructuredText with .. folio::, .. picture::, and clickable 1917 authorial endnotes.
