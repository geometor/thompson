Thompson–Hambidge–GEOMETOR Master Concordance
=============================================

.. rubric:: Thompson–Hambidge–GEOMETOR Morphological & Dynamic Symmetry Concordance

Structured cross-repository concordance mapping D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson's On Growth and Form (Cambridge University Press, 1st ed., 1917) and Jay Hambidge's The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry (Yale University Press, 1919–1920) across the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium and symbolic geometry ecosystem.

Editions Aligned & Copyright Boundary
-------------------------------------

* **thompson_1917_cup**: On Growth and Form, First Edition (Cambridge: University Press, 1917, xv + 793 pp., 17 chapters)
* **hambidge_1920_diagonal**: The Diagonal, Vol. I, Nos. 1–12 ('The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry', New Haven: Yale University Press, 1919–1920)
* **hambidge_1920_vase**: Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase (New Haven: Yale University Press, 1920)

.. note::

   Strictly anchored to the 1917 1st Edition (17 chapters) and 1919–1920 Yale editions; Thompson's 1942 2nd Edition and Bonner's 1961 abridgement are excluded.
