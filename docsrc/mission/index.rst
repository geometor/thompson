Mission
=======

``geometor.thompson`` establishes a rigorous, unabridged, folio-verified Digital Scriptorium edition of **D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson's** *On Growth and Form* (**Cambridge University Press, First Edition, 1917**) paired with **Jay Hambidge's** *The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry* (**Yale University Press, 1919–1920**) as the biological and morphological keystone of the **GEOMETOR** ecosystem.

Core Objectives
---------------

1. **1917 / 1920 Verbatim Public-Domain Fidelity**: Preserve 100% unabridged primary texts from Thompson's 17-chapter 1917 Cambridge *first edition* and Hambidge's 1919–1920 Yale editions (*The Diagonal* & *Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase*), verified directly against 300 DPI archival page scans while strictly excluding post-1920 copyrighted revisions.
2. **Unified Morphological & Constructive Concordance**: Connect biological morphogenesis directly to classical Euclidean proportion (``geometor/euclid``, ``geometor/elements``), golden sections and gnomons (``geometor/divine``), Fermat–Torricelli foam junctions ``X(13)`` (``geometor/etc``), exact rational spreads ($s = 3/4$, $s = 8/9$) and root-rectangle integer quadrances ($Q = 1\dots 5$) (``geometor/wildberger``), botanical parastichy lattices (``geometor/phyllotaxis``), and projective/affine transformation groups (``geometor/pappus``, ``geometor/hilbert``).
3. **Calibrated Geometric Plates**: Illuminate Thompson's and Hambidge's mathematical arguments with 11 standalone, theme-adaptive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG figures honoring strict content/code separation.
