Sir D’Arcy Wentworth Thompson¶

Sir D’Arcy Wentworth Thompson (1860–1948) saw living organisms as diagrams of physical forces—shaped by surface tension, scaling laws, honeycombs of minimal area, equiangular spirals, and continuous coordinate deformations. His 1917 masterpiece On Growth and Form remains the foundational bridge between classical geometry and mathematical biology. This digital archive presents his unabridged seventeen-chapter first edition in a synchronized three-track Book Codex with theme-adaptive vector plates.
