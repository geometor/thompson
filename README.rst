**Sir D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson • On Growth and Form • semantic digital archive**

Mission
-------

``geometor/thompson`` is part of the interconnected `GEOMETOR <https://geometor.com>`_ semantic digital archive. Sir D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson's landmark masterpiece *On Growth and Form* illuminated the physical forces, dynamic symmetries, and geometric invariants governing biological morphology. Within this archive, Thompson's foundational treatise is liberated from isolated print bounds into addressable semantic units, synchronized multi-track codices, calibrated theme-adaptive vector diagrams, and exact symbolic formulations across the GEOMETOR corpus.

Books in This Archive
---------------------

- `On Growth and Form <https://geometor.github.io/thompson/growth_and_form/>`_ — Sir D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson (Cambridge, 1917), presenting the unabridged first edition across seven synchronized codex units covering magnitude, cells, tissues, equiangular spirals, phyllotaxis, skeleton efficiency, and the theory of transformations.

-------------------
